Advertising & privacy

Refrax G displays third-party advertisements supplied by Adsterra. Advertisements are labeled and separate from scientific information. Advertising does not control access to the observatory.

Responsive ad placements load by default. You can hide them using the footer control; that preference is stored locally. Browser or regional privacy controls may restrict ad delivery. Third-party providers may receive network and browser information, use their own storage, and show external advertisements. The separate sponsored link opens a third-party destination only when selected.

Ad frames are restricted from directly reading the observatory's document, and geolocation, microphone and camera permissions are not granted to these frames. Browser and network privacy features can affect ad availability. The observatory does not include selected coordinates in ad requests; map providers may receive viewport requests.

To stop loading ads, return to the observatory and choose Hide advertisements. This does not delete data already held by third parties. See the advertising provider's privacy policy for information about its data handling.
