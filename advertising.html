Advertising & privacy

Refrax G offers optional third-party advertisements supplied by Adsterra. Advertisements are labeled and separate from scientific information. The observatory functions without enabling ads.

When you choose Allow advertisements, the site stores your preference locally and loads appropriately sized ad placements. Third-party providers may receive network and browser information, use their own storage, and show external advertisements. The separate sponsored link opens a third-party destination only when selected.

Ad frames are restricted from directly reading the observatory's document, and geolocation, microphone and camera permissions are not granted to these frames. Browser and network privacy features can affect ad availability. The observatory does not include selected coordinates in ad requests; map providers may receive viewport requests.

To stop loading ads, return to the observatory and choose Disable advertisements. This does not delete data already held by third parties. See the advertising provider's privacy policy for information about its data handling.
