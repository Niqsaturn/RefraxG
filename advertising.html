Advertising & privacy

Refrax G uses Google AdSense and Adsterra advertising to support the website. Advertisements are separate from scientific information and do not control access to the observatory. Ad networks may process device, browser and network information and use storage according to their policies and applicable privacy requirements.

The observatory homepage has one rotating Niqsaturn promotion, one Adsterra display banner and one Google AdSense display placement below the scientific explanation. Publisher artwork does not require third-party advertising scripts. The network placements are optional and can be enabled or disabled below the map; your selection is saved locally. Google may also present its applicable consent controls. The Adsterra banner is isolated in a sandboxed frame without top-level navigation or access to the observatory. Network availability, privacy choices and publisher approval may affect delivery. The preference control is not a substitute for a certified consent management platform where one is required.

The observatory itself runs in a separate cross-origin frame. The website does not pass selected geographic coordinates to advertising code. Map data providers may receive map viewport requests when you explore an area. The advertising providers may independently receive network information; refer to their privacy policies for details.
