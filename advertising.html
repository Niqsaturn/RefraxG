Advertising & privacy

Refrax G uses Google AdSense and Adsterra advertising to support the website. Advertisements are separate from scientific information and do not control access to the observatory. Ad networks may process device, browser and network information and use storage according to their policies and applicable privacy requirements.

On the observatory homepage, Niqsaturn's own image advertisements are visible without loading a third-party advertising script. Optional third-party display placements can be enabled or disabled using the controls below the map; the selection is saved locally in your browser. These placements are isolated in sandboxed frames. No popunder, notification permission or automatic navigation is used by the homepage ad integration. Scientific guide pages may use their existing network advertising and provider consent controls. Browser settings, ad blockers, network restrictions and publisher approval may affect delivery. Do not confuse advertisements with scientific tools or data.

The observatory itself runs in a separate cross-origin frame. The website does not pass selected geographic coordinates to advertising code. Map data providers may receive map viewport requests when you explore an area. The advertising providers may independently receive network information; refer to their privacy policies for details.
