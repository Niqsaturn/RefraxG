Advertising & privacy

Refrax G uses Google AdSense and Adsterra advertising to support the website. Advertisements are separate from scientific information and do not control access to the observatory. Ad networks may process device, browser and network information and use storage according to their policies and applicable privacy requirements.

The observatory homepage has one rotating Niqsaturn banner, one responsive Adsterra display placement and one Google AdSense placement below the scientific explanation. Publisher artwork does not require third-party scripts. Network advertisements are requested automatically as appropriate; provider consent controls and applicable privacy requirements remain in effect. The Adsterra display frame is sandboxed and cannot navigate the observatory or access its map controls. The website does not request browser permissions for advertising. Network availability, privacy choices and publisher approval may affect delivery.

The observatory itself runs in a separate cross-origin frame. The website does not pass selected geographic coordinates to advertising code. Map data providers may receive map viewport requests when you explore an area. The advertising providers may independently receive network information; refer to their privacy policies for details.
